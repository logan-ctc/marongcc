Who we follow

Jesus

Who is He?

Jesus was born about 2,000 years ago in a rural town called Bethlehem. His arrival and the life that followed were far from ordinary. He preached a message of love, grace and salvation, performed many miracles, and claimed to be the King — and thousands gathered to hear Him. Those claims unsettled the authorities, who had Him crucified. But the story doesn't end there: three days later He rose from the dead and appeared to hundreds before ascending into heaven. What they tried to snuff out was only the beginning.

Why did He come?

Jesus came into the world for one very simple reason: to rescue humanity from its sinful nature and open a way for us all to be reconciled to God. Yes, He performed miracles, spoke wise words, and even went fishing at times — but all of it pointed to the One who sent Him: God.

Why does it matter to me?

Jesus made some very big claims. If He was simply a deluded man wandering around 2,000 years ago, we can dismiss everything He said. But if His claims are true, then we each face a choice — to accept Him as King of our lives, or to go our own way. And if they're true, the only way to restore our relationship with God is by accepting Jesus as Lord and Saviour.

If you'd like to know more, we'd love to chat.
